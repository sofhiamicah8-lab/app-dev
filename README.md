# My Favorite Series: The Big Bang Theory

## Overview
**The Big Bang Theory** is a popular American sitcom created by Chuck Lorre and Bill Prady that follows a group of brilliant scientist and their socially awkward interactions with the world around them.

---

## Key Details
* **Genre:** Sitcom, Comedy
* **Main Characters:**
  1. Sheldon Cooper
  2. Leonard Hofstadter
  3. Penny
  4. Howard Wolowitz
  5. Rajesh Koothrappali

---

## Why I Like It
> "Bazinga!" — *Sheldon Cooper*

* **Great Humor:** It blends geek culture, scientific references, and classic sitcom comedy effortlessly.
* **Character Growth:** Watching characters like Sheldon evolve socially over several seasons is heartwarming.
* **Relatable Dynamic:** The strong bond and loyalty among the friend group make it feel like family.
